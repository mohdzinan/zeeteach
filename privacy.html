ZEE TEACH · SITE INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy describes the ZeeTeach site as it is currently built. The site presents an economics lesson and does not provide user accounts or a form for submitting personal information.

Information the site handles

The presentation runs in your browser. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, or account details. The site code does not set cookies, run analytics, or send slide activity to a tracking service.

When you use Share or copy a link, your browser opens its own sharing interface or clipboard permission flow after your action. Print and save-slide features run in your browser. ZeeTeach does not receive the contents of your clipboard or files you print or save.

Server and hosting

The web server returns the page, lesson data, and illustrations. The hosting provider or network operator may process connection information, such as an IP address and request time, in their infrastructure logs. Their own privacy terms govern those systems.

Third-party services

The current site does not load analytics, advertising, or remote font services. Your browser and operating system may still provide built-in sharing, clipboard, and printing services when you choose those features.

Choices and questions

You can browse the lesson without creating an account. You can decline clipboard access or close your device’s sharing or printing interface. For a question about this installation or its hosting, contact the teacher or site administrator who shared the site with you.

Policy updates

This page will be updated if the site’s data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision of this page.
